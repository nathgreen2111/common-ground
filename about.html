I’ve sat where you’re sitting.

Common Ground is run by Nathan Green, a director who has negotiated his own business through lender pressure and turned it around.

I’m Nathan. I’m 22, and I opened my first business at 18. Today I run a multi-site leisure group with around 40 staff, and we picked up an industry award along the way.

Growing fast meant borrowing: bank loans, asset finance, cash advances. The trading was strong, but there came a point where the repayments were stacking up faster than the cash was coming in.

What I found was that there’s very little help between “keep going and hope” and calling an insolvency practitioner. So I did it myself. I sat down with the numbers, wrote to every lender, laid out our position honestly and agreed plans the business could actually stick to.

We turned it around. Common Ground exists to do the same for other directors.

I’m not an insolvency practitioner, and I don’t pretend to be. What I am is someone who’s been on your side of the table, knows how lenders make decisions, and will tell you straight whether your business can get through this.
