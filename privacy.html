Privacy notice

How Common Ground Advisory Ltd collects and uses your information.

Who we are

Common Ground Advisory Ltd (company number XXXXXXXX, registered office [registered office address]) is the data controller for personal information collected through this website and our services. You can contact us at hello@commongroundadvisory.co.uk.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, we collect your name, company name, email address, phone number, turnover band and anything you tell us about the business.

If you become a client, we also hold financial documents about your company, such as accounts, bank statements and finance agreements, details of any personal guarantees you’ve given, and our correspondence with your creditors.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange your call.
  • To review your company’s position and negotiate with the creditors you authorise us to contact.
  • To keep the records we are required to keep by law.

Our lawful basis

We rely on our legitimate interests to reply to enquiries, on the performance of a contract when you become a client, and on legal obligation for record keeping.

Who we share it with

  • The creditors you authorise us to deal with, and no one else without your permission.
  • Service providers who help us run the business, such as our website host (Vercel), our form provider (Formspree) and our email provider.
  • Professionals you ask us to introduce you to, such as a licensed insolvency practitioner or finance broker.

We never sell your information.

Some of our service providers store data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK–US data bridge.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don’t go any further are kept for up to 12 months. Client files are kept for six years after our work ends, to meet legal and accounting requirements.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict how we use it, or ask for a copy to take elsewhere. Email hello@commongroundadvisory.co.uk and we’ll respond within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Cookies

This website doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies.
