Who we help

We only take on businesses we believe we can genuinely help, so here’s exactly who that is.

We’re a good fit if

  • You’re a limited company, anywhere in the UK
  • Customers are buying and the day-to-day trading is sound
  • The problem is the repayments, not the business
  • Your accounts and bank statements back that up
  • You’d rather sort it now than wait for the default letters

We’re not the right people if

  • Sales have dropped away and aren’t coming back. Stretching the debt out won’t fix that, and we’ll say so.
  • You’re a sole trader or partnership. Business Debtline gives free advice.
  • Your main debt is HMRC or VAT. A Time to Pay arrangement, set up directly with HMRC, is usually the answer.
  • You need a CVA, administration or liquidation. We’ll introduce you to a licensed insolvency practitioner we trust.

What we deal with

Most businesses in this position have two or three of these stacked on top of each other. That’s where one joined-up plan makes the difference.

Bank loans
Term loans, overdrafts and government-backed loans with repayments that no longer fit your cash flow.
Merchant cash advances
Daily or weekly sweeps from your card takings, often with top-ups layered on top. We know how these lenders work.
Asset finance
Leases and hire purchase on equipment, vehicles and fit-out, including agreements you’ve already fallen behind on.
Rent arrears
Commercial rent owed to your landlord or managing agent, and a realistic plan for what gets paid from here.
Suppliers
Trade creditors who’ve put you on stop, passed you to collections or are threatening court.

Where we stop

We handle informal negotiation. Formal processes like CVAs, administration and liquidation can only be run by a licensed insolvency practitioner. If that’s what you need, we’ll tell you straight away and introduce you to one we trust.

How it works

Four steps from first call to agreed plan. Most negotiations take a few weeks.

  1. Free call

    30 minutes by phone or video. You tell us what’s going on. We tell you honestly whether we can help.

  2. Position review

    We go through your accounts, bank statements and every agreement, then set out your position and our proposed plan in writing.

  3. We talk to your creditors

    With your authority, we write to each one, lay out the numbers properly and handle the back-and-forth.

  4. Plan agreed

    New terms confirmed in writing, one clear schedule, and you get back to running the business.
