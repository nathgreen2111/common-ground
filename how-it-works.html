How it works

From first call to a plan agreed in writing. Usually a few weeks.

  1. Free call

    30 minutes by phone or video. Tell us who you owe and how trading’s going. By the end, you’ll know whether we can help.

    Free

    A straight answer.

  2. Position review

    We go through your accounts, bank statements and agreements, and work out what the business can really afford.

    £400

    Your position and plan in writing, usually within a week.

  3. We negotiate

    With your authority, we put the plan to each creditor and handle every call and email.

    £0

    Nothing to pay while we negotiate.

  4. Plan agreed

    Each creditor confirms the new terms in writing. You get one schedule to follow.

    £500

    Per creditor who agrees, spread over 6 months.

Want us to keep an eye on it afterwards? We can stay on as your point of contact for £150 a month.

What to have ready

Don’t have it all? We’ll help you pull it together.

  • Latest filed accounts
  • This year’s trading figures
  • Six months of bank statements
  • Every finance agreement
  • Letters and demands from creditors
  • Any personal guarantees

Is it right for you?

Yes, if

  • You’re a UK limited company
  • Trading is solid. The repayments aren’t.
  • You’d rather act before the default letters

No, if

  • Sales have gone and aren’t coming back
  • You’re a sole trader. Business Debtline can help for free.
  • It’s mainly HMRC or VAT. Use Time to Pay.
  • You need a formal process. We’ll refer you to a licensed insolvency practitioner.

Questions

Will lenders deal with you?

Yes, once you’ve signed a letter of authority. Most would rather agree a plan than chase a debt through court.

Should I stop paying while you negotiate?

No. Don’t stop paying anyone without talking to us first.

Will it affect my credit rating?

It can. We’ll explain the likely impact before anything is agreed. An agreed plan usually does far less damage than defaults or court claims.

I’ve signed personal guarantees. Can you help?

Yes. We factor them into every negotiation.

What if the business isn’t viable?

We’ll tell you early and introduce you to a licensed insolvency practitioner.
