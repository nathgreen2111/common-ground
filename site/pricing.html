Questions about fees

Why do you charge for the position review?

Because it’s proper work: going through every agreement and statement and building a plan that will stand up to a lender’s scrutiny. The written review is yours to keep, and some directors use it to go back to their bank or accountant themselves.

What if a creditor won’t agree?

Some won’t at first. We keep going until each creditor has given a final answer, at no extra cost. If one flatly refuses, we’ll talk you through your options, including whether a formal process would protect you better.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes. The negotiation fee can be spread over three monthly payments, and the optional monitoring is billed monthly.

Do you get paid by lenders or insolvency practitioners?

We don’t take payments from the creditors we’re negotiating with. If we introduce you to a finance provider or broker and they pay us a commission, we’ll tell you the amount and deduct it from our fee. If we refer you to an insolvency practitioner, we’ll tell you whether any referral fee is involved.

The sooner you call, the more options you have.

Lenders are usually more flexible before things reach defaults and court claims. The first 30 minutes cost nothing.
