How it works

No jargon and no surprises. Here’s exactly what happens, from the first call to a plan agreed in writing.

  1. The free call

    30 minutes by phone or video. You tell us who you owe, what they’re asking for and how the business is trading. We’ll ask some direct questions.

    By the end of the call you’ll know whether we think we can help. If we can’t, we’ll point you to someone who can.

    You get

    A straight answer. No cost, no obligation.

  2. Position review

    You send us your accounts, bank statements and every finance agreement (there’s a full list below). We work out what the business can realistically afford each month, and what a fair offer to each creditor looks like.

    You get

    A written summary of where you stand and the plan we’d propose, usually within a week of receiving your documents. It’s yours to keep, whatever you decide.

  3. We talk to your creditors

    You sign a letter of authority so we can deal with each creditor for you. We write to them with the numbers set out properly, propose a plan and handle the calls and emails, so you’re not fielding them while trying to run the business.

    Creditors are far more likely to agree when they can see the full picture and a plan that’s clearly affordable.

    You get

    One point of contact for every creditor, and regular updates on where each one stands.

  4. Plan agreed

    Every change is confirmed in writing. You get one schedule showing who gets paid what, and when.

    You get

    Written confirmation from each creditor and a repayment schedule you can actually stick to.

  5. Staying on track Optional

    If you want us to, we stay on as the point of contact for your creditors. If a month goes wrong, we speak to them before it turns into a problem.

    You get

    Someone keeping an eye on the plan for a small monthly fee. Cancel anytime.

What to have ready

Don’t worry if you haven’t got all of it. We’ll tell you what’s missing and help you pull it together.

  • Your latest filed accounts
  • Management accounts or this year’s trading figures
  • Six months of business bank statements
  • Every loan, lease, hire purchase and cash advance agreement
  • Letters, emails and demands from creditors
  • Your lease and rent statement, if you owe rent
  • Details of any personal guarantees you’ve signed
  • A list of who you owe, if you have one (we can build it with you)

What we don’t do

Being clear about this protects you, and means we only take on work we can genuinely help with.

Formal insolvency
CVAs, administration and liquidation can only be handled by a licensed insolvency practitioner. If we think that’s where you are, we’ll tell you on the first call and introduce you to one we trust.
HMRC and VAT
We don’t usually take these on. HMRC runs its own Time to Pay arrangements and you can often set one up directly. If HMRC is one of several creditors, we’ll build it into your overall plan.
Sole traders and partnerships
We only work with limited companies. Business Debtline offers free advice for the self-employed.
Regulated advice
We negotiate business debts. We don’t give regulated financial advice, investment advice or legal advice.

Questions directors ask us

Will my lenders actually deal with you?

Yes, once you’ve signed a letter of authority. Most creditors would rather agree a plan that gets them paid than chase a debt through collections or court. A clear, properly evidenced proposal gives them a reason to say yes.

Should I stop paying while you negotiate?

Don’t stop paying anyone without speaking to us first. If the business genuinely can’t keep up with everything, we’ll help you work out a sensible approach and put it to your creditors properly, in writing.

Will this affect my company’s credit rating?

It can, depending on the lender and what’s agreed. We’ll talk you through the likely impact before anything is signed. In most cases an agreed plan does far less damage than defaults, court judgments or insolvency.

I’ve signed personal guarantees. Can you still help?

Yes. Plenty of directors have. We factor any guarantees into the negotiations, because you’re on the hook too.

How long does it take?

The position review is usually done within a week of receiving your documents. Negotiations depend on how many creditors are involved and how quickly they respond, but most take a few weeks.

What if you think the business isn’t viable?

We’ll tell you, clearly and early. Stretching out debt for a business that can’t recover only makes things worse for you and your creditors. We’ll introduce you to a licensed insolvency practitioner so you can get proper advice on your options.

Can you help us refinance?

Sometimes the best fix is replacing expensive debt, like stacked cash advances, with a longer-term loan. We work with lenders and brokers we trust and will only suggest it when it genuinely leaves you better off. Any commission is declared upfront and knocked off our fee.

Is this confidential?

Yes. We only speak to the creditors you’ve authorised us to deal with, and we never share your information with anyone else without your permission.

The sooner you call, the more options you have.

Lenders are usually more flexible before things reach defaults and court claims. The first 30 minutes cost nothing.
